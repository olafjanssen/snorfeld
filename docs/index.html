Snorfeld

A Reluctant AI Writing Companion

Snorfeld is a desktop writing assistant that provides real-time feedback on your writing and insights in your content. Snorfeld connects to local large language models (via Ollama) to analyze your text and offer suggestions for grammar, style, and structure improvements.

Unlike eager writing tools that generates content, Snorfeld gives you direct, actionable corrections — helping you improve your writing through honest, reluctant analysis.

"Because your writing requires you writing."

Core Features

Real-Time Paragraph Analysis

Analyzes each paragraph as you write or navigate through your document. Displays color-coded diffs showing suggested changes. Click on any suggestion to instantly apply it to your text.

Story Bible

During writing, your story bible is continuously updated based on your new content, giving you insight into the characters, locations, objects and plot themes.

AI Integration

  • Local LLM Support: Connects to Ollama for privacy-focused, offline-capable AI analysis
  • Configurable Models: Use any Ollama-compatible model (default: qwen3.5:9b)
  • Customizable Settings: Adjust temperature, max tokens, and API endpoints
